About me

I study Mechanical Engineering (Aeronautics) at ITA, the Aeronautics Institute of Technology in Brazil, and I have just started a Master's on dual-fuel hydrogen and diesel engines.

Most of my hands-on engineering happens at Formula ITA, the university's Formula SAE Electric team, where I lead the powertrain subteam. I like work that ties a model to a measurement: write the simulation, run the test, then find out why the two disagree. I am working toward a career in powertrain and vehicle performance engineering, with Formula 1 as the long-term goal.
